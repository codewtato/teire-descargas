Shared notes with your friends, live

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • You edit the same note at the same time.
  • Lists and whiteboards that update on their own.
  • Free for Windows, Android, iPhone, Linux and browser.
  • No ads.

Write in the same note, at the same time

Share a note with a friend by their @username and watch them type, letter by letter. There's nothing to reload and no versions to send over WhatsApp: what one person changes, everyone sees.

Get Teire for freeUse it in your browser

Lists everyone fills in together

The grocery list, what to pack for the trip, the household chores. Each person ticks off their own from their phone or computer, and each card shows how much is left.

Whiteboards to think together

A whiteboard is a full-screen canvas. You see what others draw as they draw it: it's good for sketching a plan, a diagram or explaining something without writing three paragraphs.

A place for your group: the Space

The Space is a shared canvas where you put notes, lists, whiteboards, files, a chat and a calendar, and work together live. You can invite anyone with a link: they join without an account and without installing anything. The link lasts 24 hours and you can close it sooner.

How to share a note

  1. Install Teire or open it in your browser and create your free account.
  2. Add your friend by their @username.
  3. Open a note and share it. From then on, you both edit it.

It works wherever you are

Teire is available for Windows, Android, iPhone and Linux, and you can also use it from the browser without installing anything. It works offline and syncs everything when the connection comes back. On iPhone it installs from Safari: see the guide.

Your notes are still yours

Teire has no ads and doesn't sell data. PIN-protected notes are encrypted on your device before being uploaded: even the server can't read them. What you don't share, nobody else sees. The details are in the privacy policy.
