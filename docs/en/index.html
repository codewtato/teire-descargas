FAQ

What people ask us most.

Is Teire free?

Yes, and everything important is free. Teire Premium is optional: it adds themes, colors and more Teire AI requests.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account your notes stay on your device only. With one they sync across your devices and you can share them. You sign in with email, Google, Discord or GitHub.

Is it just a notes app?

No: it's an organizer. It has notes, but also tasks, reminders, a calendar, focus mode and everything you share with your people.

Does the island work on Linux and on phones?

The island is Windows-only. On Linux and Android you get the rest of Teire; on Android, use Share and pick Teire from any app. With the web version you can use it from any browser.

What do you do with my data?

We don't show ads or use analytics, and PIN notes are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I use it on iPhone, or without installing anything?

Yes. Open useteire.app/app in Safari and add it to your home screen: it gets its own icon like any app, and it doesn't go through the App Store. It also works in any browser, but the island and Teire AI voice aren't there.

Why does Windows show a warning?

Because the installer doesn't have a paid code signature yet, and Windows distrusts apps with few downloads. It doesn't mean it has a virus: click “More info” and then “Run anyway”.

How do I invite someone to a Space?

In your Space tap Share and create a link. Whoever opens it joins without an account and without installing anything. The link lasts 24 hours and you can close it sooner.

Is Teire available in English? What about Teire AI?

The app is in Spanish and English, picked from your device's language (you can change it in Settings). Teire AI, the voice and commands, works in Spanish only for now.
