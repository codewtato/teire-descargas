Teire on your iPhone

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • It installs from Safari and it's free, with no App Store.
  • Sign in from the icon, not from Safari.
  • Notifications turn on with one tap.
  • It works from iOS 16.4.

Install it in three taps

Open Teire in Safari
  1. Open Teire in Safari with the button above. If this link opened another browser, copy it and paste it into Safari: useteire.app/app
  2. Tap Share (the little square with an arrow pointing up). On newer versions of iOS it may be inside the ··· button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Close Safari and open Teire from its icon. That's it: it now works like any other app.

Sign in to your account

Sign in from the icon, not from Safari. iOS keeps the two separate: if you sign in on Safari, the installed app doesn't know about it.

If you already have an account with an email, sign in as usual. Your notes, friends and chats are there.

Do you sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub? Those buttons aren't on iPhone, because they would take you out of the app. Tap “Sign in with an email code”, enter the email of that account and choose a password. It's the same account, and you'll still be able to sign in with Google on your other devices.

Turn on notifications

The first time you open it, Teire will ask whether you want to turn them on. Tap Turn on and then Allow. They tell you about messages, notes shared with you and reminders, even when the app is closed.

If you said “Not now”, you can turn them on from Profile → Notifications. You need iOS 16.4 or newer.

Save links

On iPhone, Teire can't check what you copied on its own. The simplest way is this:

  1. Copy the link from Instagram, TikTok or anywhere else.
  2. Open Teire and go to Saved.
  3. Tap Paste and confirm in the little bubble the iPhone shows.

Save from Share, without opening Teire

It's optional and you set it up only once. After that, from any app you tap Share → Save to Teire and it's saved. You need an account and the Shortcuts app, which comes with the iPhone.

  1. In Teire, go to Profile → Save from other apps, tap Generate code and copy it. Don't give it to anyone.
  2. Open Shortcuts, tap + and name it Save to Teire.
  3. Add the Get Contents of URL action and paste this address:

  4. Tap the arrow on that action and choose the POST method and JSON as the request body.
  5. Add a text field with the key codigo and paste your code as the value.
  6. Add another text field with the key link and, as the value, choose the Shortcut Input variable.
  7. Add the Show Notification action and set Contents of URL as the text. That way you can see whether it was saved.
  8. Tap the shortcut's info icon and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Save it.

The names may vary a little depending on your version of iOS. If you change the code in Teire, update it in the shortcut too.

What's different on iPhone

  • Everything important is there: notes, lists, whiteboards, reminders, Saved, friends, chat, Space and Premium.
  • It updates itself: when there's a new version, the app picks it up when you open it with internet.
  • There's no island or Teire AI voice: the island is a Windows feature and the voice is desktop-only.
  • It doesn't show up in Share on its own: iOS doesn't allow it for apps installed from Safari. That's why the shortcut exists.
  • There's no notice when you copy: on Android, Teire offers to save what you copied. On iPhone you tap Paste.

If something doesn't work

  • I don't see “Add to Home Screen”. Make sure you're in Safari. In the Share list, scroll to the bottom or tap “Edit Actions”.
  • It asks me to sign in again. You signed in on Safari. Open Teire from the icon and sign in there.
  • I'm not getting notifications. Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Teire, and that Focus mode isn't blocking them.
  • The shortcut says the code isn't valid. Generate a new one in Teire and paste it into the shortcut.
  • It still doesn't work. Write to us from Help, inside the app, or at codewtato@gmail.com.
