An alternative to Google Keep

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • Notes, lists and whiteboards you edit together, live.
  • Free and without ads.
  • It doesn't import your Keep notes yet: you move them over by hand.

What Teire adds

  • Live notes and lists: you see how your friends type, letter by letter.
  • Full-screen whiteboards: several people draw together and you see it as it happens.
  • The Space: a place for your group, with chat, a whiteboard and what you share.
  • Saved: reels, links, texts and screenshots, grouped by day and separate from your notes.
  • The island on Windows: drop a link or a photo on it from any program and it's saved.
  • PIN notes: they're encrypted on your device, and even the server can't read them.
  • No ads: everything important is free and Premium is optional.
Get Teire for freeUse it in your browser

Where you can use it

There are apps for Windows, Android and Linux, it installs on iPhone from Safari and it also works in the browser. With a free account, your notes sync across all your devices.

What to know before you switch

  • There's no importer yet. Your Keep notes are moved by hand, copying and pasting. If you have hundreds, start with the ones you use.
  • On iPhone it doesn't go through the App Store. It installs from Safari in three taps.
  • The island is Windows-only and Teire AI's voice is for the desktop app.
  • Teire is an independent project made in Argentina, not a big company.

When it makes sense to stay with Keep

If you live inside Google and move your notes to Docs all the time, Keep is already integrated with those services. If what you want is to share with your people, build live lists and have a place for your group, Teire is made for that.
