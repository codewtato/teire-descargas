Right of withdrawal

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Courtesy translation. Teire is run from Argentina and these texts are governed by Argentine law: if the English and the Spanish versions differ, the Spanish version prevails. Read the Spanish version.

The short version

  • You have 10 calendar days from the payment.
  • You don't have to give a reason.
  • We refund everything through the same payment method.

What it is

If you paid for Teire Premium or a Premium gift, you have 10 calendar days from the payment to withdraw. You don't have to give any reason and it costs you nothing: we refund everything you paid. It's a right given to you by Argentina's Consumer Protection Law No. 24,240, and this page is here because of Resolution 424/2020.

How to request it

  1. Write to us at codewtato@gmail.com with the subject “Withdrawal”. Tell us your Teire username, the date of the payment and whether you paid with Mercado Pago or PayPal.
  2. Within 24 hours we send you a case code, so you can follow your request.
  3. We refund the money through the same method you paid with, and we cancel the subscription so it isn't charged again.

If you just don't want it to renew

If more than 10 days have passed or you just don't want next month to be charged, cancel the subscription from the Premium section of the app. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.
