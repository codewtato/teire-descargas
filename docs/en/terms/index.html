What you write is yours

Your notes, drawings, messages and everything you upload remain yours. For Teire to work, you give us permission to store it, copy it between your devices and show it to the people you share it with. That permission ends when you delete it or delete your account, except for what others have already copied.

What you draw in the Community goes on a shared canvas: other people can cover it or draw over it, and it may remain visible even if you delete your account.

If someone doesn't follow these rules, we may delete that content and suspend or close their account. If you see something like that, let us know from Help.
