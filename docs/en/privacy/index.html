Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Courtesy translation. Teire is run from Argentina and these texts are governed by Argentine law: if the English and the Spanish versions differ, the Spanish version prevails. Read the Spanish version.

The short version

  • Without an account, your notes stay on your device only.
  • With an account, we store what's needed to sync and share.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data.
  • PIN-protected notes are encrypted on your device: we can't read them.
  • What you ask Teire AI is processed by Google Gemini.

Who looks after your data

Teire is an app made by Matías D'Agostino, an independent creator from Argentina. He is responsible for the data stored when you use Teire with an account. For any question about your data, write to codewtato@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Teire app (Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone) and to this website.

If you use Teire without an account

You can use Teire without creating an account. In that case your notes stay on your device only and aren't sent to any server of ours. If you uninstall the app or delete its data, those notes are lost.

Some features need an account and can't be used without one: sharing, the Space, chat, the Community and Teire AI.

What we store if you have an account

So your things are on all your devices and you can share them, we store on our servers:

  • Your account: your email, your username, your display name and your profile photo. If you sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub, they give us your email and your name.
  • What you create: notes, lists, whiteboards, categories, reminders, calendar tasks, audio, images and attached files, and the places you add to a note.
  • Saved items: the links, texts and images you save from the island, the clipboard or the Share menu.
  • Your people: your friends, your groups, chat messages with their photos, and the invitations you create.
  • Your Spaces: what you put on the canvas (notes, lists, whiteboards, files up to 25 MB, chat and the Space's calendar), who you share it with, and a 14-day history so you can recover what was removed.
  • Your activity in Teire: whether you're online and your status (Online, Away, Do not disturb or Invisible), the last time you signed in and your badges, like the connection streak. To know whether features are used, we note per day whether you had the island on or entered a Space and how many times, but not what you do there.
  • Premium: if you have Premium, until when, the gifts you sent or received, and the payment identifier given to us by Mercado Pago or PayPal. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your preferences: theme, colors, language, island settings and similar.

If you join a Space with a link

Anyone who opens a guest link joins that Space without an account. To do that we create an anonymous session with a random name you can change. We store that name, what the person does in the Space (their cursor, strokes and messages) and, for the link, only a fingerprint (not the link itself). The link lasts 24 hours or until it's closed, and anonymous sessions with no live link are deleted automatically after 2 days.

What stays on your device

  • PIN-protected notes: they're encrypted on your device before being uploaded. We only store the encrypted text and can't read it. If you forget the PIN, there's no way to recover them.
  • “Oye Teire”: the app recognizes those words on your computer, without sending audio anywhere. Only when it hears them (or when you press Ctrl K) does it record what you ask and send it to Teire AI.
  • The island, the clipboard and screenshots: Teire checks on your computer whether you copied a link or took a screenshot, to offer to save it. Nothing is sent until you tap Save.
  • The island's music: the island reads on your computer what's playing (the track, the artist and the cover) and doesn't send it to our servers. To show the lyrics, the app asks LRCLIB (lrclib.net) for the track's lyrics using its title, artist and duration and, if it isn't there, lyrics.ovh.
  • Focus and notes pinned to the desktop: what you write as your focus and the position of pinned notes stay on your device.
  • Files you drag into a note: except for images, they aren't uploaded. Only the file name is kept.

What we use your data for

  • To make the app work: store and sync your things, share them with whoever you choose and send you the notifications you asked for.
  • To charge for Premium and deliver gifts.
  • To look after Teire: prevent abuse and spam, and set daily usage limits, for example on Teire AI requests.
  • To reply when you write to us from Help.

We don't show ads, we don't sell your data and we don't use analytics or tracking tools, neither in the app nor on this website.

We also share data if a law or a competent authority requires it.

What other people see

  • Your username, photo, badges and status are seen by your friends and by people who share a Space or a group with you.
  • A shared note is seen and edited by the people you shared it with, and they can see who is typing.
  • The Community is public: what you draw on the canvas can be seen by anyone with an account, and it shows that you took part.
  • Chat, group and Space messages are seen by the people in that conversation.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have your account. Notes you delete go to the trash until you delete them for good.

If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days. We only keep what the law requires us to keep, such as payment receipts. Until we add a button to delete your account from the app, you can request it by writing to codewtato@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326, you can ask us to see, correct, update or delete your data, free of charge. Write to us at codewtato@gmail.com. If you ask to see it, we'll answer within 10 calendar days at most; if you ask to correct or delete it, within 5 business days at most.

The holder of the personal data may exercise the right of access free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown, as established in article 14, paragraph 3 of Law No. 25,326. The Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the supervisory authority of Law No. 25,326, has the power to handle complaints and claims filed by those whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules in force.

How we protect it

Connections are encrypted, each person can only read their own things and what has been shared with them (the database rules enforce this), and the keys to the services live on the server, never in the app. No system is perfect: if something happens that affects your data, we'll let you know.

Minors

Teire isn't intended for children under 13. If you're under 18, use it with the permission of your parents or a responsible adult.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. The fonts and animations are hosted right here. To show you the latest version, the page asks GitHub which one it is. The theme (light or dark) and the language you choose are remembered in your browser and aren't sent anywhere. GitHub, which hosts the site, may log your IP address.

If this policy changes

If we change something important, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect, and we'll update the date above. If you have questions, write to us at codewtato@gmail.com.
